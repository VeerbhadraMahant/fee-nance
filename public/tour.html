Fee-Nance

One ledger for your own money and the money you share with friends. Built for India: rupees, UPI, bank CSV statements, and it keeps working with no signal.

Personal budgets and insights Group splits to the paisa Offline, then syncs AI copilot on your own data

How to use this tour

  • Scroll down, or jump with the numbered chips at the top.
  • Everything on the right of each step is live. Click, type, drag.
  • Flip the Online switch (top right) to go offline, then add a transaction.
  • This is a guided replica with made-up data, not the production app. Step 11 says plainly what is finished and what is not.

2Dashboard

Where you stand today

Balance, trend, where the money went, and a health score built from five measurable pillars.

What to try
  • Change the range.
  • Turn group shares off and watch every number move. Your share of a group bill is real spending, so it counts by default.
3Transactions

Log it in seconds

Add, edit, filter. Bring in months of history from a bank statement CSV. Recurring entries such as rent and salary post themselves.

What to try
  • Add a purchase.
  • Open Import CSV: it maps columns, previews, and skips what you already have.
  • Notice the two identical Swiggy charges. Step 6 catches them.
4Budgets

Pace, not just totals

"67% spent" means little on day 5. Fee-Nance compares spending with how much of the month has passed and projects where you will land.

What to try
  • Drag the day of the month.
  • Food includes your share of group dinners and groceries, not just what you paid.
5Groups and splits

Split bills to the paisa

Equal, percentage, custom or itemized with GST. The rupees always add up exactly, and the app suggests the fewest payments to settle.

What to try
  • Add a Dinner expense under each tab.
  • In Custom, make the amounts not add up. It is refused.
  • Tap Pay via UPI, then Mark paid.
6Insights

It notices for you

Duplicate charges, unusual spends, overlapping subscriptions, category drift, and a 90-day cash forecast with a confidence band.

What to try

7Tax planner

Indian income tax for FY 2026-27 with both regimes side by side, a recommendation, and how many extra deductions it would take for the old one to win.

What to try

8Goals

What if I save more?

Savings goals with a projected finish date. Move the monthly amount and see the date change.

What to try

9Copilot

Ask your own ledger

Questions are answered from your data through read-only tools, and every answer shows which tools it used. It can also draft an entry from a sentence, but nothing is saved until you press Add.

About this demo
  • Replies here are scripted so the tour works without a key. The real copilot uses Gemini with the same tools and a rate limit of 30 questions an hour.
10Works offline

No signal, no problem

Install it on a phone. With no connection, you can still log transactions. They wait on the device and are sent, in order and exactly once, when you are back online. Nothing is sent if nothing changed.

What to try
  • Switch to Offline (top right) and add two transactions.
  • Tick "Lose the first reply", then go online.
